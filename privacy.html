Privacy policy

Last updated 1 October 2026

Who we are

RenewalGuard ("we", "us") is a subscription and bill reminder service available on the web, iPhone and Android. Questions about this policy can be sent to cris@fortunastreams.com.

No bank access

RenewalGuard never connects to your bank, payment card, or financial accounts. You add subscription details manually.

What we store

We store your account email and the subscription names, categories, costs, dates, reminder preferences, links, notes and optional icons you choose to enter.

How data is used

Your data is used only to provide your tracker and reminders. It is not sold or shared with third parties for any other purpose. Each account can access only its own subscription records.

Phone notifications

Phone reminders are scheduled on your device. Your notification time and appearance preferences are stored on your device only.

Third-party services

Your account and subscription data are hosted by our app platform provider, which processes it on our behalf to run the service. Reminder emails are sent through a third-party email delivery provider, which processes only what's needed to deliver the email (your email address and the reminder content). Service logos shown in the app are loaded from a public favicon service using the service's web address only — never your personal data. In-app purchases, when available, are handled by Apple or Google; we do not receive your card details.

Your choices

You can pause reminders, export your subscription data on a Paid plan, or permanently delete your account and associated data from Account Settings in the app or on the web.

Data deletion

When you delete your account from Account Settings, all of your data — including your subscriptions and reminder history — is permanently and immediately removed, not just deactivated. You can request deletion at any time using the "Delete my account" option; there is no need to contact support.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to how it is used. Email us and we'll respond within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at cris@fortunastreams.com.
