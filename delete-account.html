Delete your RenewalGuard account

Permanently remove your account and all of its data.

Delete in the app (quickest)

1. Open RenewalGuard on your phone and go to the Account tab.
2. Scroll to Delete account and tap Delete my account.
3. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Yes, delete everything.

Delete on the web

Log in at the RenewalGuard web app, open Account settings and choose Delete my account.

Can't log in?

Email cris@fortunastreams.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my RenewalGuard account". We'll confirm and delete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Your account, email address, every subscription you've added (names, costs, dates, notes, links and icons), your reminder settings and your reminder history. Deletion is permanent and immediate — it can't be undone. We don't keep any of this data after deletion.
