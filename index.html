FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need to connect my bank?

No — never. You add your subscriptions yourself, so RenewalGuard has no access to your bank, cards or transactions. No Open Banking, no reconnecting every 90 days.

What if I miss a notification?

Reminders come by phone notification and email, so you have two chances to catch them. You can send yourself a test alert any time, and snooze a reminder or mark a bill paid once it's dealt with.

Isn't it daft to pay a subscription to track subscriptions?

We agree — that's why RenewalGuard is free for up to 5 subscriptions, with reminders included. A Paid plan for unlimited subscriptions and custom reminder timing is coming soon, and it's entirely optional.

What happens if I change phone?

Your list is saved to your RenewalGuard account, not just your phone. Log in on a new phone or in your web browser and everything is there.

Does it work with UK services?

Yes — it's built in the UK, in £, with 80+ popular UK services ready to pick: streaming, broadband, mobile, energy, gyms, council tax, TV licence and more. Anything else, just type the name.

Can RenewalGuard cancel subscriptions for me?

No — you stay in control. Save each service's cancellation link and open it in one tap when you're ready.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Go to Account settings → Delete account and everything is removed permanently and immediately.
